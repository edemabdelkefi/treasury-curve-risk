# Data provenance

Source: Federal Reserve Board, Gürkaynak, Sack and Wright nominal Treasury yield-curve estimates.

Landing page: https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm

CSV: https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/yield-curve-tables/feds200628.csv

Snapshot downloaded on 2026-09-30. The source's research-product notice is preserved in the first CSV line. Only complete observations from 1990 through 2025 enter the study. All columns outside SVENY01-SVENY30 and later observations are excluded from the analysis. No download occurs during reproduction.

The complete snapshot includes other maturities, fitted quantities and dates for provenance. The snapshot hash and the analysis window are recorded in results/metrics.json.
